## Corroboration and retrieval notes

- Additional discovery searches were made for deep-time settlement, writing, printing, declarations, vaccination, independence, international institutions and spaceflight. They were used to locate inspectable passages, not to treat search snippets as verification.
- UNESCO and a BnF overview both described Jikji’s 1377 metal-type printing. This discovery check does not resolve the wider scholarly and language-review questions; the public record remains an evidence-checked draft.
- Some IWM, UN landing-page, NHCP and UK National Archives image-page requests failed. Failed pages were not accepted as supporting citations. An accessible UN treaty-depositary entry supplied the Charter dates; other inaccessible candidates were omitted or replaced with inspectable events.
- WHO institutional pages corroborate parts of its own vaccination chronology but remain one source family. UNESCO pages likewise count as one family.
- Calendar and interpretation concerns are recorded in event date notes and `review.unresolvedIssues`. No absence of a recorded dispute should be read as scholarly consensus.
- A next editorial pass should prioritize a genuinely independent source for each record, regional-language sources, civilian and Indigenous perspectives, and independent experts for calendar conversions.
